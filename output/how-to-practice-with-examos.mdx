# How to Practice for IT Certifications on ExamOS

ExamOS has four quiz types, three difficulty modes, and one readiness score that ties them together. New users usually start with the free sample, then wonder how the rest fits. This guide covers what each quiz type is for, how modes change difficulty, how the readiness score is calculated, and how streaks, rest days, timezones, and reminders work.

## Start Here: What to Do Today

The pattern that works is unglamorous. Sprint daily, cover every domain, take a Mock Exam when you want a real signal, and watch the readiness score rather than any single session result.

| Frequency | Activity | Purpose |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| Daily | Daily Sprint (7 min) | Keep the streak; cover every domain |
| A few times a week | Bonus Quest | Extra volume on a weak domain |
| Every 1-2 weeks | Mock Exam | Test under full-length conditions |
| Weekly | Review Insights | Find the weakest domain and concepts |

The goal is not to grind questions. It is to keep a streak, cover every domain and concept, and let the readiness score reflect that work.

## Activate the Exams You Are Preparing For

Daily Sprints and Mock Exams run against an *active* exam. You can keep up to **5 exams active at once**. Once you reach the cap, you have to mark an exam inactive in Settings before you can start practicing a new one.

Any quiz activity on an exam reactivates it automatically. If you step away for a while and come back, starting a session is enough to bring it back into your active set.

## Quiz Types

### Free Practice Exam (Preview)

The preview quiz is the no-account sample. It pulls **10-20 questions** (depending on how many domains the exam has) from a restricted pool of easy and medium single-answer questions, and shows an explanation after each one.

- Use it to judge question quality and difficulty before signing up.
- It is **not** counted in your readiness score.
- If you create an account during or after a preview, that attempt is migrated into your history and used to pick your first Daily Sprint domain (your weakest one).

### Daily Sprint

The Daily Sprint is the habit-building quiz, and the main reason the streak exists.

- **7 questions in 7 minutes**, focused on a single domain.
- **Always free**, and available **once per active exam per day**.
- Questions are ordered easiest to hardest. Difficulty labels are internal and never shown.
- It counts toward your streak if you answer **at least 4 of the 7** questions.
- Pass threshold is **70%** (5 of 7). Scoring **86% or higher** (6 of 7) awards a **+3 credit** bonus.
- It auto-submits when the timer runs out. If you start within 30 minutes of local midnight, the deadline is extended, with a hard cap of 15 minutes past midnight.

The domain is chosen for you rather than picked at random. That selection is explained below.

### Bonus Quest

A Bonus Quest is extra practice you take on top of the daily sprint.

- Costs **1 credit** and does not affect your streak.
- You choose the domain and the mode.
- It counts toward your readiness score, but with less weight than a Mock Exam.

Use it when you have finished your sprint and want more volume on a specific domain.

### Mock Exam (Deep Practice)

The Mock Exam is the full-length test.

- **30 questions in 30 minutes**, covering all domains weighted according to the official exam blueprint.
- Run in a mode of your choice (Rookie, Challenger, or Legend).
- Completing a Mock Exam also advances your Daily Sprint from Phase 1 to Phase 2 (covered below).
- It carries the **highest weight** in the readiness score because it is the closest simulation of the real exam.

## Modes

Modes change the difficulty mix of the questions you receive. They are not separate question banks; the same exam content is drawn at different difficulty ratios.

| Mode | Difficulty mix | Cost |
|------|----------------|------|
| Rookie | 30% easy / 40% medium / 30% hard | Free |
| Challenger | 10% easy / 30% medium / 60% hard | 1 credit |
| Legend | 0% easy / 10% medium / 90% hard | 2 credits |

Rookie is capped at **2 passes per exam** by default. Use it to establish a baseline, not to farm easy scores. Once you have a foundation, Challenger and Legend give a more honest signal.

Rookie is where you build vocabulary and confirm foundations. Challenger tests whether you understand how concepts connect. Legend removes the easy eliminations and forces reasoning through layered scenarios.

## How the Daily Sprint Picks Your Domain

Domain selection runs in two phases, tracked per exam.

### Phase 1: Coverage Rotation

The goal of Phase 1 is to make sure no domain is skipped.

- Each day, the system suggests a domain you have not yet covered.
- You can override the suggestion, but you cannot pick a domain already completed in Phase 1.
- **All Phase 1 sprints run in Rookie mode.**
- Your very first sprint uses whichever of these applies: the weakest domain from a recent preview quiz, the weakest domain from a recent Mock Exam, or the highest-weight domain if neither exists.
- Phase 1 ends when every domain has been covered at least once. Exams with only two domains finish Phase 1 after day two.

### Phase 2: Targeted Practice

Once every domain has been covered, the system stops rotating and starts targeting. It favors domains that are weak, stale, or heavily weighted on the exam.

The system also caps the same domain at **3 consecutive sprints**, so a single weak area cannot monopolise your practice.

**Phase 2 runs in Challenger mode by default.** Two mode suggestions can appear:

- If your rolling average for a domain reaches **80%**, the system suggests moving that domain to **Legend**.
- If it drops below **70%** on Legend, it suggests moving back to **Challenger**.

You can accept or dismiss either suggestion, and you can change modes yourself from the quiz start screen.

## The Readiness Score

The readiness score is a single number from **0 to 100** for a specific exam. It updates after every completed quiz. Preview quizzes are excluded.

Two things drive it more than anything else:

**Coverage.** If you have not practiced every domain at least once, your score is capped. A learner at 85% on two of five domains is not close to ready, even if those two scores are strong. This is the single most common reason a score looks lower than expected.

**Recency and volume.** Domains you have not practiced in a while lose weight. So does a small question count. Answering 40 questions well means less than answering 400 questions at the same accuracy. The confidence figure next to your score (roughly ±3 to ±10) tells you how much to trust it. A wider range means less volume.

Mock Exams count more than Daily Sprints, and Daily Sprints count more than Bonus Quests. The score is not a simple average of your session results.

### What the Percentages Mean

| Readiness Score | Label | What to do |
|-----------------|-------|------------|
| 0-40 | Not ready | Build foundations; keep Rookie Daily Sprints going |
| 41-60 | Needs significant work | Focus on weak domains; do not skip coverage |
| 61-75 | Borderline | Target weak domains and add Mock Exams |
| 76-85 | Almost Ready | Book soon; keep the streak and cover remaining gaps |
| 86-100 | Exam Ready | You are prepared; book the exam |

## Streaks and Rest Days

ExamOS tracks two streaks: a **global streak** across all your exams, and a **per-exam streak**. Both advance when you complete a qualifying session on a given day. Daily Sprints and Mock Exams qualify; Bonus Quests do not.

- Missing a qualifying session on a normal day resets your streak.
- You can configure up to **2 rest days per week**. Missing a session on a rest day does not break your streak.
- Practicing on a rest day still counts and still advances the streak.
- Milestones at **3, 7, 14, 30, 60, and 100 days** trigger credit awards and share prompts.

Rest days exist so a realistic schedule does not punish you for having a life. Use them deliberately.

## Timezones and Reminders

All streaks, daily resets, and deadlines use **your local time**, based on the timezone set in your profile. Set it before you start so a sprint does not expire at an unexpected hour.

Daily Sprint reminders work like this:

- One reminder per exam per day, sent at your preferred time. The default is **7:00 pm local**, and you can change it in Settings.
- A reminder is only sent if you have **not** already completed a qualifying session for that exam that day.
- No reminder is sent on your rest days.
- You can turn practice reminders off entirely in your notification preferences.

## FAQ

### Why is my readiness score lower than my practice scores?

Almost always coverage. If you have only practiced two of five domains, the coverage gate holds the score down. The fix is to cover the remaining domains, not to repeat the ones you are already good at.

### How many questions should I answer before trusting the score?

More than one session. The confidence figure narrows as volume grows. Treat a score built on a few dozen questions as directional, not final.

### Do I have to take a Daily Sprint every day?

You can practice whenever you like, but the streak only advances on days you complete a qualifying session. Rest days are there for the days you cannot.

### Can I choose my own difficulty?

Yes. You choose the mode for Mock Exams and Bonus Quests. During Phase 1, Daily Sprints are fixed to Rookie, and Phase 2 defaults to Challenger with Legend suggested when a domain average reaches 80%.
