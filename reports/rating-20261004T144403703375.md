## Strengths
1. Certification sequence is sound and well justified: optional CDL, ACE as the operational foundation, PDE as the core credential, then PCA/PML as clearly labelled specializations.
2. Technical specificity is strong across the steps (Storage Write API, Beam windowing and triggers, Dataproc Serverless, Dataplex quality rules and lineage, BigQuery column-level security, Cloud DLP de-identification, VPC Service Controls).
3. Each step ships concrete project ideas and a checkpoint, and the honestTimeline table gives both minimum and realistic pacing per credential combination.

## Weaknesses
1. The PDE domain names and weightings are wrong and the incorrect breakdown is repeated in the description, both step summaries, the notes, and the closing section, so the error propagates through the whole roadmap.
2. SEO metadata misses the spec on three counts: excerpt title is 40 characters (below the 50 minimum), seoTitle runs about 79 characters (over the 70 cap), and seoDescription runs about 177 characters (over the 160 target).
3. No exam mechanics are given for PDE or ACE (cost, question count, time limit, passing threshold), and the body contains only target roles and employer categories, so the roadmap's substance lives entirely in frontmatter.

## Factual Issues
- PDE domain structure is incorrect. The post states 'Design (30%), Build (25%), Operationalize (20%), ML Integration (15%), Security/Governance (10%)'. Google's official Professional Data Engineer exam guide sections are Designing data processing systems, Ingesting and processing the data, Storing the data, Preparing and using data for analysis, and Maintaining and automating data workloads. There is no 'Operationalize' or 'ML Integration' domain, and the 30%/25%/55% weighting claim that is repeated in the description, Step 2, Step 3, the notes and finalSectionBody does not match the published weights.
- Step 2 lists 'BigQueryOperator' as a Cloud Composer operator. That operator is deprecated in Airflow 2 and superseded by BigQueryInsertJobOperator; the name will mislead readers on current Cloud Composer versions.

## Summary
A technically rich and well-sequenced ACE to PDE roadmap undermined by an incorrect PDE domain breakdown that is repeated throughout the document, plus SEO metadata that misses every length target and a handful of em dash tells in the prose.
